After a detailed deliberations on the conduct of court proceedings,
the Authority decides to adopt the following procedure.

A. PREPARATION OF FINAL ORDER AND ITS PRONOUNCEMENT.

1) WHERE THE CASE IS reserved for orders, with the approval
of chairman file could be sent to the designated Member for
the preparation of the draft orders.

a) The designated Member is required to prepare the draft
order within a period of 15 days from the receipt of the
file. -

After the orders are drafted by the designated
Member, he will submit the file to the Chairman for
perusal and for on word transmission to the remaining
other Member to ascertain his views/ Signature.

b) After the receipt of the draft order, the receiving

member shall resubmit the file within a period of 7
days with his signature or with his dissenting note to
the Chairperson.
The Chairperson will finalise the draft after considering
the views of both the members within a period of 7
days of the receipt of the file. Therefore after the
conclusion of the hearing, orders shall be pronounced
by the Authority within a period of 30 days.

c) Neither the Chairman nor Members will record any
notings of dissent or any comment of administrative
nature on the “Court File’. The note sheet of the court
file shall only contain the day to day proceedings
conducted in the court hall as recorded by the
presiding officer (Chairman / Member). - As far as

2) A person of the rank of at least a section officer may be
designated as a court officer to assist the court proceedings.
Following could be the functions of the court officer.

i) He will be the custodian of all the court files and he will
also handle all the court files before and after hearing.

ii) Before the first date of hearing of a complaint by the
Authority, the court officer will fill up information in the
annexure (attached to this SOP) and submit it along with
the complaint in the court file for perusal of the
Authority.

iii) To prepare in advance (at least by 4 pm of the previous
day) the cause list of the cases to be heard the next day.
Serve a copy in the personal section of the Chairperson
and the Members and ensure that the cause list is
uploaded on the Authorities website.

iv) To place a copy of the cause list on the notice board
within 10:30am of the date of hearing.

v) To check and verify that the documents / statements /
memos / rejoinders / affidavits / vakalatnamas as
ordered in the earlier date of hearing have been filed. If
not to give a note on the correspondence portion of the
file.

vi) To ensure the attendance of the complainants /
promoters on the date of hearing.

vii) After the day’s court proceedings, he will carry out the
follow up action of the day’s proceedings, by issuing
notices / summons to the parties concerned.

Care should be taken to draft the notice / summons in
accordance with the instructions given in the court hall
by the Authority.

Where the instructions of the Authority as recorded in
the court file is a direction to the parties to produce
certain documents, the same instructions have to be
complied with office notice of the Court Officer, and the
other staff members as the case may be. Only the copy
of the notice issued in pursuance of the instructions of
the Authority shall be placed in the court file.

After the Chairman and the Members have signed the
final orders, the Court Officer will ensure that it is put on
the website and also communicate to the respective
parties.

The Court Officer will also be in charge of the issue of
certified copies.

Wherever appeals are preferred against the orders of the
Authority, the Court Officer will ensure that the files are
sent in time to the Tribunal / the High Court.

The Court Officer will also ensure for a_ legal
representation before the Appellate Authorities with the
approval of the Chairman.

The Court Officer shall collect and compile land
mark/important judgements of the Supreme Court, High
Courts and of other Real Estate Regulatory Authorities.
This shall be used by the Authority for a reference
purposes.

The Court Officer will be in charge of the Court Library.

The Court Officer can be given the assistance of a Legal
Assistant / Junior Assistants / Senior Assistants.
