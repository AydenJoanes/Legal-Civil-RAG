NOTIFICATION


Whereas the draft of Karnataka Real Estate (Regulation and Development)
Rules, 2016, was published vide Government Order No. DOH 128 KHB 2016, dated
24/10/2016 in Part-I of the Karnataka Gazette (Extra Ordinary Number 1193), dated
24/10/2016, inviting suggestions from all persons likely to be affected thereby within
fifteen days from the date of its publication in the Official Gazette,


Whereas the said Gazette was made available to public on 24/10/2016,


Whereas, in the above said draft rules, a format for ‘Agreement of Sale’ was
provided as Annexure-A under rule 8,


Whereas, the above Karnataka Real Estate (Regulation and Development)
Rules, 2017was published as in Pat-IVA of the Karnataka Gazette (Extra Ordinary
Number 662), dated 11/7/2017, in which format for ‘Agreement of Sale’ was not
included,


Whereas, again a draft format for ‘Agreement of Sale’ was published in
Notification No. DOH 184 KHB 2017, dated 10th August 2017 on the web-site of
Karnataka Real Estate Regulatory Authority i.e. rera.karnataka.gov.in, inviting
objections / suggestions, from any person within 15 days from the date of its
publication in the said web-site;


And whereas, the objections and suggestions received in respect of the said
draft have been considered by the State Government;


Now, therefore, in exercise of powers conferred by clause (h) of sub-section (2)
of section 84 read with section 13 of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development)
Act, 2016 (16 of 2016), and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, Government
of Karnataka hereby makes the following rules, namely:
1. Short title and Commencement and Application:


(1) These rules may be called the Karnataka Real Estate (Regulation and

Development) (Amendment) Rules, 2017.

(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the official

Gazette.
